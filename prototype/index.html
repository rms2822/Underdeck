Weapons (♦) dull with use — after a kill, a blade only cuts monsters of equal or lesser value.

Elixirs (♥) heal, but only the first you drink in each room.

Flee a room before acting in it — never twice in a row.

Clear all 44 cards and choose: retreat with your gold, or descend — deeper floors pay more, but dying forfeits most of your unbanked hoard.
