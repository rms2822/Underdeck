A dungeon of forty-four cards

SCROUNDEL

Monsters (♣ ♠) strike for their value. Fight bare-handed, or let your weapon take the blow.

Weapons (♦) dull with use — after a kill, a blade only cuts monsters of equal or lesser value.

Elixirs (♥) heal, but only the first you drink in each room.

Flee a room before acting in it — never twice in a row. Outlast all 44 cards and your health is your score.
